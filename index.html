Definitions Account — the organisation (e.g. UWA) Lead — the contact person at that account (e.g. Rick at UWA) Opportunity (“deal”) — a specific problem worth pursuing; one account can carry several, across teams Contact — a person linked to an account (e.g. from an event list) Asset — a piece of our proof (case study, one-pager) used in outreach Hook — the specific, provable reason we lead with Tier (A / B / defer) — priority from the 6-factor score (max 12) ◆ Decision — a fork where a human picks yes/no; a human can also pause, edit or stop at any node
